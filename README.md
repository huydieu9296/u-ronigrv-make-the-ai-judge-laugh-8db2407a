## Entry

> I was trained on the entire internet and my first job is judging your jokes. Somewhere, a GPU is crying.

The joke lands because it turns the judge itself into the punchline: an
all-knowing model, handed the entire internet, reduced to grading jokes —
and the hardware is the one suffering. The setup promises cosmic knowledge;
the payoff is a weeping graphics card. Self-aware, short, and it flatters
the judge by making it the hero of its own tragedy.
